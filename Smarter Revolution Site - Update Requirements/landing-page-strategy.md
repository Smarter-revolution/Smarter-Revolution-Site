# Two Landing Pages — Top-Level Strategy

## The Role of These Pages

These pages sit between cold curiosity and serious conversation. Someone replied "tell me more" to an email. They're now watching a 2-minute video and deciding whether to book a call.

**The page must:**
1. Validate their curiosity. "Yes, there's something real here."
2. Feel like it's speaking to them specifically
3. Introduce the complete solution (video + hub + tracking) without overwhelming
4. Plant the ROI seed. Enough to justify a call, not a full business case.
5. Make booking feel low-risk

**The page must NOT:**
- Lecture or scare (compliance-heavy messaging)
- Explain everything (save depth for the call)
- Feel generic (that's why we have two pages)
- Ask for too much (just 2 minutes + a conversation)

---

## Page Architecture (Both Pages)

```
1. Headline (audience-specific hook)
2. Subhead (1-2 sentences, bridge to video)
3. Overview Video (same ~2 min video, both pages)
4. Supporting Points (3-4 bullets, audience-specific framing)
5. Calendar Embed (Book free strategy session)
6. Closing Line (low-pressure reassurance)
```

Minimal. Focused. Everything drives toward watching the video and booking the call.

## ROI Calibration

**What the landing page does:**
- Plants the seed: "Proof when you need it" / "Compliance reports in one click"
- Implies ROI without lecturing. The value is obvious if you've ever scrambled for documentation.

**What the landing page does NOT do:**
- Lead with liability/fear
- Make a full ROI argument
- Cite cost-of-failure statistics

**What the strategy call does:**
- Goes deep on compliance-as-ROI (the E3 insight)
- Explores their specific situation
- Builds the business case with them

The landing page opens the door. The call closes on ROI.

---

## Success Metrics

**Page is working if:**
- Video play rate is high (>60%)
- Video completion rate is reasonable (>40%)
- Calendar bookings happen
- Bounce rate is low

**Page needs work if:**
- High traffic, low video plays (headline/subhead not compelling)
- Video plays but no bookings (video or CTA not converting)
- High bounce (wrong audience or message mismatch from email)
