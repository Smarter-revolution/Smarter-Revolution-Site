## Section 7: Why Mid-Market

**[Section Headline]**
We focus on mid-market companies for a reason.

**[Copy]**
Mid-market companies ($10M–$250M) occupy a strategic position:

**Big enough** to have real content and training needs that justify investment.

**Small enough** that transformation is actually achievable—without enterprise bureaucracy slowing everything down.

**Underserved** by both enterprise solutions (too expensive, too complex) and small business tools (too limited, too basic).

**Decision-capable** with accessible leadership who can approve initiatives and champion change.

This is where we do our best work. Big enough to matter. Small enough to move.
