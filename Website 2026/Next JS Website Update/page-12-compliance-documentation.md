## Section 2: The Problem

**[Section Headline]**
Compliance shouldn't feel like a fire drill.

**[Copy]**
Audit notice arrives. Everyone panics.

"Who completed the safety training?" 
"I think most people did it... probably."

"Where's the documentation?"
"Let me check... somewhere in SharePoint... or maybe email..."

"Can you prove when they completed it?"
"Define 'prove.'"

This isn't a compliance problem. It's a documentation problem. You're probably doing the training. You're just not proving it in a way that holds up to scrutiny.

Sign-in sheets get lost. Email confirmations get deleted. "I'm pretty sure they watched it" doesn't satisfy auditors.

And when you can't prove training happened, you're exposed—legally, financially, and operationally.
