## Section 2: The Problem

**[Section Headline]**
Training shouldn't feel like a scavenger hunt.

**[Copy]**
Here's how training works at most companies:

New hire shows up. Someone emails them a link to a shared drive with 47 folders. "The training materials are in there somewhere." Good luck.

Existing employees need to learn a new process. They ask around until they find someone who knows—or they just wing it and hope for the best.

Compliance audit happens. Everyone scrambles to prove that training actually occurred. Spoiler: nobody can.

The training content itself? PDFs from 2019. PowerPoints with clip art. Videos where someone reads bullet points for 45 minutes while you check your phone.

This isn't a people problem. It's an infrastructure problem.
