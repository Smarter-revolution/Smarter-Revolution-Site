## Section 4: The Avatar Selection Process

**[Section Headline]**
See exactly what you're getting—before you commit.

**[Intro Copy]**
Most video projects start with mood boards and hope. Ours start with real output. Before any production begins, we create 3-5 short demo clips combining the actual avatar, actual voice, and actual delivery style. You see what you're getting—not a description of what you might get.

---

**[How It Works]**

**Step 1:** We produce 3-5 avatar demos (15 seconds each) based on your brand and audience.

**Step 2:** Each demo combines appearance, voice, and on-brand delivery—the full experience, not just a headshot and a voice sample.

**Step 3:** You pick your presenter. No surprises. No "I'll know it when I see it" paralysis.

**Step 4:** Avatar and voice are locked. Production moves forward with confidence.

---

**[Why This Matters]**

This isn't just a nice touch—it eliminates the single biggest cause of video project disasters: late-stage changes. When you've approved the real thing upfront, you don't get to final delivery and say "actually, can we try a different voice?" 

The result? Faster projects, fewer revisions, better outcomes.
