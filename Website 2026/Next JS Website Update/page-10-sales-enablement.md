## Section 2: The Problem

**[Section Headline]**
Channel enablement is usually a disaster. Here's why.

**[Copy]**
You've got a great product. You've got partners who could sell a lot more of it. There's just one problem: they don't actually know how to sell it.

Some partners get it. Most are guessing. And the messaging? All over the map.

Partner A emphasizes the wrong features. Partner B undersells the value. Partner C is still using the pitch deck from three product versions ago.

Meanwhile, your competitors have partners who sound like they actually work there.

The problem isn't partner motivation—it's partner enablement. They want to sell more. They just don't have what they need to do it.

Scattered training materials don't fix this. Email attachments don't fix this. Quarterly webinars definitely don't fix this.

What fixes it? Infrastructure.
