# Path 1: Video Production

## Path 1 Anchor Message

**AI-powered video production—create faster, scale smarter, look better.**

Professional corporate video without the traditional production headaches. Training videos, explainers, sales content—produced at speeds and costs that finally make comprehensive video libraries possible.

---

## Path 1 Modular Messages

### 1.1 Content Velocity

**Headline:** More video content, faster than you thought possible.

**Supporting copy:** Your content needs grow faster than your team can keep up. AI-powered production changes the math. What used to take weeks now takes days. Stop choosing which videos to make—make them all.

**Pain point:** Can't keep up with demand for video content.

**Best for:** Companies who know they need more video but are bottlenecked by time and resources.

---

### 1.2 Professional Quality, Finally Affordable

**Headline:** Hollywood-quality corporate video without Hollywood budgets.

**Supporting copy:** Traditional video production quotes made comprehensive training libraries financially impossible. One video for $15K? That's not a strategy—that's a budget crisis. AI-powered production delivers professional quality at a fraction of the cost.

**Pain point:** Priced out by traditional video agencies.

**Best for:** Companies who've received shocking quotes from video production agencies.

---

### 1.3 Modern Replaces Outdated

**Headline:** Training content people actually want to watch.

**Supporting copy:** PDFs from 2018. PowerPoints with clip art. Videos where someone reads bullet points for 45 minutes. Your team deserves better. Modern, engaging video content that reflects who your company is today.

**Pain point:** Embarrassed by current training materials.

**Best for:** Companies whose content doesn't match their brand quality.

---

### 1.4 Consistency at Scale

**Headline:** Same presenter, same quality, every single video.

**Supporting copy:** Traditional video means coordinating schedules, booking talent, hoping for consistency. AI avatars deliver the same professional presence across 5 videos or 500. Your brand stays consistent no matter how much content you need.

**Pain point:** Inconsistent quality across video library.

**Best for:** Companies needing large video libraries with uniform presentation.

---

### 1.5 Multilingual Made Simple

**Headline:** One video, every language your business needs.

**Supporting copy:** Bilingual workforce? Global customers? International partners? Create once, deploy in multiple languages—without reshooting, without hiring translators, without doubling your budget.

**Pain point:** Need multilingual content but can't afford to produce everything twice.

**Best for:** Companies with English/Spanish needs or international reach.

---

### 1.6 From Videos to System

**Headline:** Your videos deserve a better home than a shared drive.

**Supporting copy:** Scattered across SharePoint, buried in email threads, lost on someone's desktop. Great video content is worthless if nobody can find it. Deliver through a professional Hub—organized, trackable, accessible from anywhere.

**Pain point:** Video content exists but is disorganized and underutilized.

**Best for:** Companies with existing video that needs proper infrastructure. *(Leads to Hub conversation)*

---

### 1.7 Measurable Training Impact

**Headline:** Know exactly who watched what—and whether it worked.

**Supporting copy:** Training without tracking is just hope. See who completed what, where they dropped off, and how content performs. Compliance reporting in one click. ROI you can actually prove to leadership.

**Pain point:** Can't demonstrate training effectiveness or compliance.

**Best for:** Leaders who need to prove training ROI or meet compliance requirements.

---

### 1.8 Deadline-Ready

**Headline:** Need it in weeks, not months? Done.

**Supporting copy:** Product launch in 30 days. Compliance deadline next quarter. New hire class starting Monday. Traditional video production can't move that fast. AI-powered production delivers professional content on timelines that actually match how business moves.

**Pain point:** Urgent deadline, traditional production too slow.

**Best for:** Companies with time-sensitive video needs—launches, compliance, events, onboarding waves.

**Proof point available:** 10-minute training video delivered in 10 days.

# Path 2: AI-Ready Web Development

## Path 2 Anchor Message

**Modern web infrastructure—stop patching outdated tech, build what's next.**

Your website isn't just a brochure. It's business infrastructure. Built on the latest technology, integrated with AI, optimized for the new search landscape, and designed to actually convert.

---

## Path 2 Modular Messages

### 2.1 The Platform Problem

**Headline:** WordPress wasn't built for where the web is going.

**Supporting copy:** Bloated plugins. Security patches. Slow load times. Constant maintenance. WordPress was great for 2010. The web has moved on. Modern infrastructure means faster, more secure, AI-ready—without the duct tape and prayers.

**Pain point:** Frustrated with current platform limitations and maintenance.

**Best for:** Companies stuck on WordPress, Magento, Squarespace, or similar platforms.

**Villains to name:** WordPress, Magento, Squarespace, Wix, Drupal, legacy custom builds.

---

### 2.2 AI Visibility

**Headline:** AI can't recommend what AI can't understand.

**Supporting copy:** Search is changing. AI systems now influence what gets found, recommended, and trusted. If your website isn't structured for AI to read and understand, you're invisible to the systems shaping buying decisions.

**Pain point:** Concerned about visibility in AI-driven search.

**Best for:** Companies thinking about GEO, AI search, future of discovery.

---

### 2.3 Future-Proof Foundation

**Headline:** Stop patching. Start building on what's next.

**Supporting copy:** Every workaround, plugin, and patch is technical debt. At some point, rebuilding costs less than maintaining. Modern architecture means you're building forward, not constantly fixing backward.

**Pain point:** Exhausted by maintaining outdated systems.

**Best for:** Companies whose website has become a maintenance burden.

---

### 2.4 Performance + Intelligence

**Headline:** Fast, secure, and built for AI.

**Supporting copy:** Page speed matters. Security matters. AI integration matters. Modern web infrastructure delivers all three—not as afterthoughts bolted on, but as foundational architecture.

**Pain point:** Site is slow, vulnerable, or can't integrate modern tools.

**Best for:** Companies who know their site underperforms technically.

---

### 2.5 Business Infrastructure

**Headline:** Your website should work as hard as your team.

**Supporting copy:** A website isn't a digital brochure—it's business infrastructure. Lead capture, customer portals, training delivery, partner enablement, AI-powered support. Modern builds turn your web presence into operational advantage.

**Pain point:** Website is passive, doesn't drive business outcomes.

**Best for:** Companies who want their website to do more than exist.

---

### 2.6 Designed to Convert

**Headline:** Beautiful matters. Conversion matters more.

**Supporting copy:** Modern web development isn't just about looking good—it's about performing. Strategic structure, clear pathways, optimized for the actions that grow your business. Design with purpose, built to convert.

**Pain point:** Website looks okay but doesn't generate results.

**Best for:** Companies disappointed in website performance metrics.

---

### 2.7 AI Integration Ready

**Headline:** Built for the tools that are changing everything.

**Supporting copy:** AI assistants, chatbots, voice agents, automated workflows—the tools reshaping business need infrastructure that supports them. Modern architecture means you're ready to integrate what's next, not scrambling to retrofit.

**Pain point:** Can't integrate AI tools with current website.

**Best for:** Companies who want AI capabilities but are blocked by their platform.

---

### 2.8 Escape Developer Hell

**Headline:** Your developer shouldn't be your bottleneck.

**Supporting copy:** Waiting weeks for simple updates. Invoices that don't match the value. Emails that go unanswered. Your business moves fast—your web partner should too. Modern development with responsive support, transparent process, and actual urgency.

**Pain point:** Current developer is slow, expensive, or unresponsive.

**Best for:** Companies frustrated with their existing web developer or agency relationship.

**Villains to name:** Slow agencies, unresponsive freelancers, overpriced retainers with nothing to show.

# Channel Guidance

## Cold Email Outreach

**Path 1 Approach:**
Lead with pain—can't keep up with video needs, traditional production too expensive, content is outdated and scattered. Short, specific, one problem per email.

**Path 2 Approach:**
Lead with platform villain or AI visibility angle. Name their likely platform (WordPress, etc.). Short, provocative, create urgency around being left behind.

---

## Website

**Homepage:** 50/50 split featuring both paths equally. Umbrella message as hero, two clear entry points below.

**Path 1 Landing Page:** Anchor message + modular messages as scrolling sections. Video samples, process overview, CTA to see Avatar Selection demo.

**Path 2 Landing Page:** Anchor message + modular messages as scrolling sections. Before/after examples, tech credibility, CTA to discuss rebuild.

---

## LinkedIn

**Organic:** Rotate between Path 1 and Path 2 content. Test which resonates. Educational content about AI video production AND AI-ready web development.

**Outreach:** Match path to prospect's apparent need based on profile/company research.

---

## Sales Conversations

Diagnose which path is the entry point. Solve that problem first. Cross-sell emerges naturally as relationship develops—don't force it in early messaging.
