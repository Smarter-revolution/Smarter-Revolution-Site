## Section 7: How to Apply

**[Section Headline]**
The process is pretty simple.

---

**[Step 1]**
**Headline:** Send us your info
**Copy:** Email careers@smarterrevolution.com with your resume, portfolio or relevant work samples, and a note about why you're interested.

**[Step 2]**
**Headline:** We review (quickly)
**Copy:** If there's potential fit, we'll reach out within a week. If not, we'll let you know—no ghosting.

**[Step 3]**
**Headline:** Conversation, not interrogation
**Copy:** We'll have a real conversation about the work, your background, and whether there's mutual fit. No brain teasers or stress interviews.

**[Step 4]**
**Headline:** Small project (sometimes)
**Copy:** Depending on the role, we might ask you to complete a small paid project. We want to see how you actually work, and you should want to see how we actually collaborate.

**[Step 5]**
**Headline:** Decision
**Copy:** We move quickly. You'll know where you stand without waiting months.
