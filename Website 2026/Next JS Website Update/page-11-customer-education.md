## Section 2: The Problem

**[Section Headline]**
Your customers are struggling. Your support team is drowning.

**[Copy]**
Here's a pattern you might recognize:

Customer buys your product. Customer opens your product. Customer has no idea what to do next. Customer emails support.

Multiply that by hundreds. Or thousands.

Your support team spends their days answering the same questions over and over. "How do I set this up?" "Where do I find that?" "Why isn't this working?" (Spoiler: it's working—they just don't know how to use it.)

Meanwhile, powerful features go unused because nobody knows they exist. Customers churn not because your product is bad, but because they never figured out how to get value from it.

The knowledge gap isn't your customer's fault. It's yours.

They want to succeed. They just don't have what they need to do it.
