## SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM

### Header
Your Business Needs More Video Than Traditional Production Can Deliver

### Content

The math doesn't work anymore.

Your sales team needs enablement content for every product, every competitor, every objection. Your L&D team has a backlog of training that's been "planned" for two years. Marketing is supposed to feed the content machine daily. Internal comms is still sending emails nobody reads.

Traditional video production can't keep up. The timelines are too long. The budgets are too high. And by the time you get the final cut, half the content is already outdated.

But here's the real problem: most corporate video fails anyway.

Not because of production quality. Because it was designed to be *produced*, not to *perform*. Training videos that inform but don't change behavior. Sales content that showcases features but doesn't close deals. Brand videos that look great but don't build trust.

More video isn't the answer. *Better* video is. Video designed with intention, built on methodology, produced at the speed your business actually needs.

### Header
The Smart Business Video Framework

### Subhead
30 years of digital evolution, systematized.

### Content

Most video production is a black box. Brief goes in, video comes out, fingers crossed.

We built something different. The Smart Business Video Framework integrates ten disciplines across four layers:

**Foundation Layer**
Visual learning science. How the brain processes video. Why some content sticks and most doesn't.

**Production Layer**
Methodology that works. AI-powered production. The craft of making video that doesn't waste viewer attention.

**Application Layer**
Use-case expertise. Training and instructional design. Sales enablement strategy. Employee experience. Each application has its own requirements.

**Strategic Layer**
Video as business infrastructure. Content strategy for trust and authority. Building capability, not just producing projects.

This isn't theory. It's how we approach every engagement. The framework ensures that every video serves a larger purpose.
