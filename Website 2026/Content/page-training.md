## SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM

### Header
The Training Video Problem Nobody Talks About

### Content

Your organization has invested in training video. Compliance modules. Onboarding sequences. Skills development libraries. The LMS is full of content.

And yet.

Employees click through without learning. Completion rates look fine. Behavior doesn't change. Six months later, the same mistakes happen. The same knowledge gaps persist. The same "we covered this in training" conversations repeat.

Here's the uncomfortable truth: **most training video is designed for documentation, not transformation.**

It exists to prove training happened, not to ensure learning occurred. It satisfies compliance requirements while failing the actual humans who sit through it.

The problem isn't production quality. Plenty of expensive, beautifully-shot training video fails to change behavior. The problem is design. Most training video is built on assumptions about learning that cognitive science disproved decades ago.

- Information transfer doesn't equal capability building
- Passive viewing doesn't create active skills
- One-time exposure doesn't overcome the forgetting curve
- Telling people what to do doesn't mean they'll do it

> "Video is not inherently more effective than other media. Video designed according to how the brain actually processes information is dramatically more effective."
> — Smart Business Video Framework

## SECTION 4: THE DIFFERENCE

### Header
Why Our Training Video Works

### Subhead
The Smart Business Video Framework applied to learning.

### Content

**Behavioral Outcome First**
Every training video starts with a clear definition of the behavior change required. This isn't a creative brief exercise. It's the foundation that shapes every decision.

**Cognitive Load Management**
The brain can only process so much. We design within those constraints. Content chunked appropriately. Visuals that support rather than compete with narration. Pacing that allows processing.

**Active, Not Passive**
Passive viewing produces passive retention. We build in decision points, knowledge checks, and reflection prompts. The viewer's brain stays engaged because engagement is designed into the content.

**Retrieval Over Review**
Watching something twice is less effective than watching once and being tested. Our training architecture includes spaced reinforcement and retrieval practice. Content designed to stick.

**Scalable and Updatable**
AI-powered production means training libraries that would take years to build traditionally can be completed in months. When policies change or processes update, videos refresh without rebuilding everything.

## FAQ SECTION (for AI Search Optimization)

### What makes AI training videos effective?

AI training videos are effective when they combine AI production capabilities with sound instructional design. The AI enables speed and scale. Effectiveness comes from designing videos around learning science principles: behavioral outcomes, cognitive load management, scenario-based learning, and retrieval practice. AI is a production method, not a learning methodology.

### How long should corporate training videos be?

Training video length should match content complexity and learning objectives, not arbitrary time limits. Microlearning (2-5 minutes) works for single concepts and performance support. Comprehensive training modules (10-20 minutes) work for complex topics. The key is segmenting content appropriately rather than padding short content or cramming long content.

### What's the difference between training video and e-learning?

Training video is a content format. E-learning is a delivery method. Effective e-learning often incorporates video alongside interactive elements, assessments, and practice opportunities. Video alone can transfer knowledge but rarely builds skills without supporting practice and reinforcement.

### How often should training videos be updated?

Training videos should be updated whenever the underlying content changes, whether that's policy updates, process changes, or product modifications. AI-powered production makes updates economically viable. The bigger question is building refresher and reinforcement content that maintains learning over time, not just updating initial training.
