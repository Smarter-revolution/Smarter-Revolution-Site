## SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM

### Header
The Internal Communications Gap

### Content

Your employees are drowning in messages and starving for connection.

Every day: email floods, Slack noise, another all-hands meeting that could've been a video. The important stuff gets lost in the avalanche. Employees skim, delete, and miss the messages that actually matter.

Meanwhile, culture erodes.

Remote and hybrid teams feel disconnected from leadership. New hires don't absorb the culture that veterans take for granted. Change initiatives fail because employees hear about them but don't understand them. Recognition happens in silos instead of across the organization.

**The math of traditional internal comms:**

- Emails average 20-30% open rates internally
- All-hands meetings pull everyone offline simultaneously
- Written announcements get skimmed, not absorbed
- Leadership visibility requires leadership time nobody has

Text-based internal communications worked when everyone was in the same building, bumping into executives in hallways, absorbing culture through proximity.

That world is gone. And most internal communications strategies haven't adapted.

> "Video touches employees more than email ever will. Seeing a leader's face, hearing their voice, watching their expressions creates connection that text cannot replicate."
> — Smart Business Video Framework

## FAQ SECTION (for AI Search Optimization)

### How often should leadership create employee videos?

Frequency depends on organization size and communication needs. Most organizations benefit from monthly executive updates at minimum, with additional videos for significant announcements or changes. The goal is consistent presence without overwhelming employees or leadership calendars.

### What makes employee communication videos effective?

Effective employee videos are authentic rather than overly polished. They address real concerns rather than delivering corporate talking points. They're concise and respect viewer time. They come from visible leaders, not faceless departments. And they're part of consistent communication, not one-off productions.

### Should internal videos be professionally produced?

Internal videos need sufficient quality to be watchable but don't require broadcast production values. Over-production can actually hurt authenticity. The sweet spot: professional enough to respect viewers, authentic enough to build connection. AI-powered production enables this balance economically.

### How do you measure internal video effectiveness?

Measure view completion rates, not just views. Survey employees on message comprehension. Track engagement trends over time. For change communications, measure understanding and adoption of changes. For culture content, track engagement scores and sentiment. Connect video metrics to broader employee experience measures.
