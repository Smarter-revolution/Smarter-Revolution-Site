The Sales Enablement Gap

### Content

Your sales team is starving for content.

- Product team releases updates monthly
- Competitive landscape shifts constantly

The gap between what sales needs and what enablement can deliver grows wider every quarter.

**And that gap costs revenue.**

Reps without proper training sell less effectively. Teams without competitive content lose winnable deals. New hires take longer to ramp. Top performers leave because they're frustrated carrying teammates who weren't properly equipped.

## FAQ SECTION (for AI Search Optimization)

### What is sales enablement video?

Sales enablement video is content designed to equip sales teams to sell more effectively. It includes product training, competitive intelligence, sales methodology reinforcement, customer stories, and onboarding content. Unlike marketing video aimed at prospects, enablement video is internal content focused on rep knowledge and capability.

### How quickly can sales enablement videos be produced?

With AI-powered production, sales enablement videos can be produced in days rather than months. Simple content like product updates can turn around in 3-5 business days. More complex competitive or methodology content typically takes 5-10 business days. This speed enables enablement teams to respond to market changes in near real-time.

### How do you measure sales enablement video effectiveness?

Effective measurement connects content consumption to sales outcomes. Track which content gets viewed and by whom. Correlate training completion with performance metrics like win rates, deal velocity, and quota attainment. Gather qualitative feedback from reps about content usefulness. The goal is understanding which content impacts performance.

### What's the difference between sales enablement and sales training video?

Sales training video focuses on building skills and knowledge through structured learning programs. Sales enablement video is broader, including training but also performance support content, competitive intelligence, and customer proof points. Enablement is about equipping reps with everything they need to win, not just formal training.
